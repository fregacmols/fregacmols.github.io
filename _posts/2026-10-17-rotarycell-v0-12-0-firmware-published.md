Firmware 0.12.0 is now published! This update adds a reorganized maintenance interface, incoming and outgoing call filters, and persistent history for the latest 100 calls.

Other additions include manual and scheduled Do Not Disturb, time-zone settings, configurable maintenance Wi-Fi credentials with dial-code recovery, and adjustable idle power saving.

The development build ran on two phones for several days without notable problems. I’ve removed the -dev label and disabled the optional INA226/microSD diagnostic logger for the public build. That version is now running on a phone and appears to be working normally.

Source and OTA download

Future fixes and minor feature updates will stay in the 0.12 series.
