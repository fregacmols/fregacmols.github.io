RotaryCell has reached the point where the breadboard and hand-wired audio interface can begin giving way to purpose-built hardware.

The Rev A2 passive audio interface PCB has been laid out, checked, and sent for fabrication. The board handles the audio connection between the cellular hardware and the telephone interface while keeping the design deliberately simple.

The next PCB work will focus on a carrier for the Silvertel AG1171 SLIC. Together, the two boards should make the internal installation considerably cleaner and more repeatable than the current prototype wiring.

There is still plenty of testing ahead once the boards arrive, so Rev A2 should be considered a prototype rather than a finished production design.
