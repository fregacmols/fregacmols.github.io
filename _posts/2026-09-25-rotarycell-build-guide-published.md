The illustrated RotaryCell build guide is now public. It follows the current
PCB build from parts and board ordering through printed mounts, LilyGO
preparation, wiring, firmware, installation, audio adjustment, and final
testing.

The guide documents the Audio/Reset A4 and AG1171 carrier boards used in the
completed phones. It also includes a full wiring overview, photographs,
downloadable board files, firmware, and printable mounts.

[Open the illustrated RotaryCell build guide →](/RotaryCell/)

The guide will continue to improve as people build from it and point out the
places where another photograph or a clearer explanation would help. The
working material and engineering history remain available in the
[GitHub repository](https://github.com/fregacmols/RotaryCell).
